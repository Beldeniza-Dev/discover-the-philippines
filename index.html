A country like no other

More than just islands

The Philippines is an archipelago in Southeast Asia, located in the western Pacific Ocean. It is known for its beautiful beaches, green mountains, colorful festivals, diverse languages, and friendly communities.

Its people are shaped by many influences and have built a culture that values family, community, creativity, and hospitality. Every island and region has its own special story to share.

A well-loved dish commonly cooked with meat, vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and spices.

Our shared story

A brief look at Philippine history

Long before Spanish rule, communities across the islands had their own leaders, trade networks, beliefs, and cultures. Spain began colonizing the islands in the 1500s. The Filipino struggle for freedom grew through generations of resistance and reform.

The Philippines declared independence from Spain on June 12, 1898. The country later experienced American rule and Japanese occupation during World War II. It became an independent republic on July 4, 1946. Today, Filipinos continue to shape the nation's future.
